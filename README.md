# Xavier Laforgue

## Quantum control scientist | Scientific Software Engineer · PhD in Physics

I design control pulses that make quantum operations fast, high-fidelity and robust to the errors of real hardware: robust inverse optimization, composite and shaped pulses, adiabatic passage.

## Research

- **Optimal ultrarobust quantum gates by inverse optimization**, Phys. Rev. A 109, 062613 (2024):
  single-qubit gates robust to high order in amplitude error, 16–40 % faster than the best composite pulses.
  [HAL](https://hal.science/hal-04627274)
- **Optimal robust stimulated Raman exact passage by inverse optimization**, Phys. Rev. A 105, 032807 (2022):
  3× lower loss and a 13× wider high-fidelity window than the non-robust optimum.
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07631)
- **PhD thesis**: *Robust control for quantum technology and quantum information processing* (2022).
  [HAL](https://theses.hal.science/tel-03852413)

All 6 publications: [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4379-1581)
